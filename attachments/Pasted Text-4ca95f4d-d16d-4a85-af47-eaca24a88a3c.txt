**CRIMSON SOVEREIGN**  
**Full Open-World Map – Step-by-Step Layout**  
*(Copy-paste ready design document. Matches the art style, Six Names cycle, and Type VII palace exactly. Scale is open-world: the entire grounds + interior are one continuous space you can roam freely after spawn.)*

---

### Overall Scale & Feel
- The playable area is a large, continuous open grounds surrounding a floating black-crystal palace.
- No loading zones between outdoor areas. The only “gate” is the main palace entrance (opens after 4 outer Names).
- Ground is polished black crystal veined with slow red energy. Sky is permanent crimson storm with red lightning. A massive scarred moon hangs behind the highest spires.
- Verticality is strong: floating platforms, sky stairs, high ledges, and deep canal cuts.
- Everything is explorable the moment you spawn. Enemies, pickups, and runes are placed so you can take any order.

---

### Step 1 – The Threshold (Spawn Point)
You appear on a wide, slightly raised platform of black crystal facing the palace.

**What you see immediately**
- Straight ahead (south-to-north): the Grand Staircase rising toward the sealed palace gate.
- Left (west): low black-glass ridge leading to the floating hieroglyph archive.
- Right (east): gentle slope down into the red-canal network.
- Behind you (south): the rift you came through (still open, soft red glow). Beyond it is empty void — no playable space.
- Far north, behind the palace spires: the ember-pylon field (Vaelith).
- Far northwest, rising above the western ridge: the sky platforms and Night Ascendant climb (Nyxara).

**Immediate landmarks**
- Two ammo crates and one vital pickup on the Threshold edges.
- Soft red path markers (living runes on the ground) point toward all four outer zones if you look closely.
- First wraiths and a couple of distant sentinels are already visible.

**Player freedom**: Turn any direction and start walking. No tutorial walls.

---

### Step 2 – Central Axis – The Grand Staircase & Palace Approach
A long, wide staircase of living black stone runs from the Threshold straight up to the main gate.

**Layout**
- ~120–150 metres long, rising in three broad tiers.
- Each tier has side ledges and small side paths that loop around.
- Red energy veins pulse up the centre of the stairs.
- At the top: the massive gothic gate (sealed until four outer runes are claimed). When open it floods the stairs with deep red light.

**Side content**
- Ammo/vital pickups on the side ledges.
- Occasional shade ambushes from the sides.
- Perfect sight-lines for the Spark Rifle once you have it.

This is the main “highway” of the map but never forced.

---

### Step 3 – Northern Zone – Vaelith, First Flame
**Location**: Directly north of the palace, beyond the rear spires. You reach it by going around either side of the palace or climbing past the gate once open.

**Layout (open field)**
- Large irregular plain of cracked black crystal.
- 7–9 tall ember pylons (3–5 storeys high) scattered across the area. They leak white-hot sparks and cast long red-orange light.
- Central largest pylon holds the Spark Rune (climbable base + short interior ledge).
- Small rocky outcrops and fallen crystal shards for cover.
- Soft red rivers of ember flow between some pylons.

**Connections**
- South → back to palace rear / Grand Staircase top.
- Southwest → short ridge path into the western archive (Rynara).
- Southeast → open ground leading toward the eastern canals.

**Enemy density**: Medium-high. Wraiths around pylons, sentinels on high ledges of the palace rear, a few constructs once the gate is open.

**Pickups**: Ammo crates near three outer pylons, vital pickup at the central pylon base.

---

### Step 4 – Western Zone – Rynara, Weaver of Runes
**Location**: West of the Threshold and Grand Staircase, behind a low black-glass ridge.

**Layout (floating archive)**
- Wide shallow basin filled with slowly orbiting hieroglyph monoliths (tall black stones covered in living golden-red law runes).
- Central “knot” of pure black stone floating slightly above the ground — this holds the Law Rune.
- Monoliths rotate on fixed paths; some can be used as moving cover.
- Low crystal walls and broken columns around the outer edge create natural corridors.
- Soft red mist hangs near the ground.

**Connections**
- East → short climb over the ridge back to Threshold / Grand Staircase.
- North → rising path toward the sky platforms (Nyxara).
- Northeast → side path that skirts the palace and reaches Vaelith.

**Enemy density**: Medium. Shades love the moving monoliths. Constructs patrol the outer ring. A few sentinels on the ridge above.

**Pickups**: Two ammo crates among the outer monoliths, one vital near the central knot.

---

### Step 5 – Eastern Zone – Sanguara, Blood of the Cosmos
**Location**: East of the Threshold, sloping downward.

**Layout (canal network)**
- Three wide, silent red canals that converge at a central deep pool.
- The Tide Rune floats above a black-glass platform in the middle of the confluence.
- Narrow walkways and crystal bridges cross the canals.
- Banks are lined with low crystal reeds and occasional ankh-shaped stones.
- The liquid surface is mirror-smooth until disturbed; shapes move underneath.

**Connections**
- West → gentle slope back to Threshold.
- Northwest → path that curves around the palace base toward Vaelith.
- North → higher ground leading to the eastern side of the Grand Staircase.

**Enemy density**: High. Wraiths rise directly from the canals. Shades use the reflective surface for flanking. Sentinels stand on the far banks.

**Pickups**: Ammo crates on the three main banks, vital pickup on the central platform (risky).

---

### Step 6 – Northwestern Sky Rise – Nyxara, Night Ascendant
**Location**: High above the western ridge, northwest of the palace.

**Layout (vertical platforms)**
- Series of floating black-stone platforms connected by bridges of solidified red lightning and narrow stairs that only appear when viewed from the correct angle.
- Platforms range from small ledges to wide plazas.
- Highest platform holds the Night Rune (dark glyph that drinks light).
- Some platforms tilt or partially dissolve after a few seconds — forces movement.
- Deep drop-offs between platforms (fall damage or death if you miss).

**Connections**
- Bottom of the rise connects directly to the western archive (Rynara).
- Mid-level side bridges can reach the upper western side of the palace once the gate is open.
- Top has a long sight-line across the entire map.

**Enemy density**: Medium but vertical. Shades drop from above or rise from below. One large sentinel guards the final approach.

**Pickups**: Ammo on two mid platforms, vital on the highest platform.

---

### Step 7 – Palace Interior – Eryndra, Eternal Throne (Gated)
**Access**: Only after the four outer runes are claimed. The main gate opens with a deep red flood of light.

**Layout (multi-level gothic interior)**
- Vast circular throne chamber at the centre.
- Multiple vertical levels connected by spiral stairs, floating platforms, and side balconies.
- Walls covered in shifting hieroglyphs and living ankh-stones that drift through the air.
- High windows of pure red energy look out onto the storm.
- Side corridors and alcoves loop around the main chamber (still part of the continuous space — no loading).
- The Eternal Throne itself sits in the exact centre: a knot of black crystal and red light that looks half-chair, half-wound.

**Enemy density**: High. Higher-order constructs, continuous wraith/shade waves, vertical ambushes.

**Pickups**: Several ammo and vital crates on the side levels and balconies.

---

### Step 8 – Throne Heart – Aelith, Final Form
**Location**: The centre of the Eryndra chamber once the fifth Name is taken.

**Layout during the fight**
- The chamber expands and the walls partially draw back.
- Floating ankh-stones align into a large circle.
- Temporary rifts and rising/falling platforms appear as the fight progresses.
- The throne unfolds into Aelith’s arena.
- Full vertical space remains usable — you can still run the balconies and side paths.

After the fight the chamber returns to a quieter, still-explorable state.

### Quick Navigation Summary (Player Mental Map)
```
                    [Nyxara Sky Platforms]
                           ↑
        [Rynara Archive] ← Ridge ← [Threshold Spawn] → [Sanguara Canals]
                           ↓               ↓
                    [Vaelith Ember Field]  [Grand Staircase]
                                              ↓
                                    [Palace Gate → Eryndra Interior]
                                              ↓
                                       [Aelith Throne Heart]
```

- Everything outdoor is one continuous mesh.
- You can circle the entire palace, climb high, drop into canals, or stay on the central stairs.
- Once the gate opens, the interior becomes part of the same continuous space.

---

### Design Notes for Implementation
- Use large open meshes with natural cover (pylons, monoliths, canal banks, floating platforms).
- Red energy veins and living runes act as subtle path markers without being UI.
- Verticality and moving/temporary platforms keep movement interesting.
- Enemy placement scales with how many Names the player has already claimed.
- All six Name locations are visible from a distance once you know what to look for (glowing pylon, orbiting monoliths, glowing canal platform, dark high platform, throne knot, final form).

This is the complete open-world map of **CRIMSON SOVEREIGN**.  
You can drop straight into any zone from the Threshold and the world stays fully connected the entire run.
