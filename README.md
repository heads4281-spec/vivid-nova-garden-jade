## Play

Enter the palace, claim the Names, open BAG / MAP / rites. Desktop: pointer lock, WASD, Space jump, Shift sprint, click to fire, F to hear, M for holo atlas. Mobile: left stick + FIRE / JUMP / SPRINT / HEAR / MAP.

Sign in with email and password, Google, or X to bind claimed Names across devices. Guests still walk the grounds.
